The basics

What is a ristretto?

A shorter, more concentrated espresso: the same dose of coffee with about half the water. Sweeter, thicker and less bitter.

03 / 08 @coffee_extracts

By the numbers

92°

The ideal water temperature, in Celsius.

Hotter water over-extracts and turns bitter. Cooler water leaves the shot sour and thin.

04 / 08 @coffee_extracts

How to

Dial in your espresso

  1. Grind

    Medium-fine, like table salt.

  2. Dose

    18 g of coffee in, 36 g of espresso out.

  3. Pull

    25 to 30 seconds from the first drop.

Side by side

Moka pot vs. espresso

Moka pot

  • Stovetop, about 1.5 bar
  • Bold and slightly bitter
  • No crema

Espresso

  • Pump, 9 bar
  • Dense, syrupy body
  • Hazelnut crema
06 / 08 @coffee_extracts

Myth busting

Myth

Dark roast has more caffeine.

Fact

Roast level barely changes caffeine. Measured by the scoop, light roast actually packs a little more.

07 / 08 @coffee_extracts

Barista tip

Warm your cup before pulling the shot.

A cold cup steals heat and dulls the aroma within seconds.
