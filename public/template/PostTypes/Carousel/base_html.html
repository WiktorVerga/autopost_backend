Myth busting

Myth

You should tamp the coffee down hard.

Fact

Not in a moka pot: just level it. Packed coffee makes it hard for water to pass, and the result tastes bitter and burnt.

How to

Filling it, in order

  1. 01

    Water

    In the bottom chamber, just below the valve.

  2. 02

    Coffee

    In the filter basket, no tamping.

  3. 03

    Level

    Wipe stray grounds off the rim.

  4. 04

    Close

    Screw it shut firmly, without forcing it.

Why the valve matters
